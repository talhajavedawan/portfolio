MSc dissertation · 2026

Evaluating the customised ERP solution as an engineering artefact.

Research approach

A qualitative single-case evaluation of Customised ERP Solution against six areas: accounting fit, process integration, organisational fit, security, software quality and long-term modernisation. The study used code and system evidence and involved no human participants.

Findings and recommendations

The evaluation recognised the ERP’s connected transaction and accounting workflows, while identifying architectural and maintainability risks. It proposed a staged modernisation roadmap to strengthen testing, data integrity, application structure and access control while preserving the existing business knowledge.
