Research

Evaluating ERP fit and modernisation.

Research grounded in the accounting and operational challenges I encountered in ERP work.

MSc dissertation · 2026Qualitative case study

Evaluating the customised ERP solution as an engineering artefact

A qualitative single-case evaluation using code and system evidence. The study assessed the ERP across six areas: accounting fit, process integration, organisational fit, security, software quality and long-term modernisation. It involved no human participants.

Research focus

  • How well the system supports connected accounting and business workflows.
  • Security, software quality and maintainability as the system evolves.
  • Modernisation options that preserve established business knowledge.

Findings and recommendations

The evaluation recognised the value of connected transaction and accounting workflows while identifying architectural and maintainability risks. It proposed a staged roadmap to strengthen testing, data integrity, application structure and access control.

Related Research Methods proposal

Proposed a more accessible, modular ERP approach for accounting and finance SMEs, covering bookkeeping, procurement, inventory and administrative expenses, with role-based access and approval workflows. Interviews, surveys and evaluation were proposed methods, not completed activities or claims about a launched product.
